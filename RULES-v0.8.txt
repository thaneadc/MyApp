======================================================================
0. GAME OVERVIEW
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

Title:
Captain's Dash: The Final Isle

Players:
2–4

Genre:
Competitive pirate adventure / worker placement / mission selection /
push-your-luck / resource progression

Core objective:
- Build a capable pirate crew
- Gain Gold, Supply, Treasure, Crew and Veteran Crew
- Progress through Zone I, Zone II and Zone III
- Use Zone III to prepare for the Final Isle
- Complete a Final Mission to win immediately

Shared-loss condition:
- Each game uses 5 Final Mission cards drawn from a pool of 8
- A failed Final Mission is permanently discarded
- If all 5 Final Missions are discarded and nobody has succeeded:
  ALL PLAYERS LOSE

Design intent:
- Mission completion should be the primary engine for progression
- Pirate Haven should be used for preparation, recruitment and recovery
- Players should not need to repeatedly farm Gold/Supply for many turns
- Final Isle should normally require a strong team containing multiple
  Veteran Crew and Treasure cards
- Final Mission should remain risky even for a well-prepared player


======================================================================
1. BOARD / NAVIGATION MODEL
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

There is no island-number sequence.
There are no route lines between islands.
There is no forced movement path.

When a Zone is unlocked:
- A player may choose any currently face-up Mission in that Zone
- Player Pirate Token moves from Pirate Haven to the chosen Mission
- Resolve the Expedition
- Token returns to Pirate Haven after resolution

All players use the same pirate portrait token design.
Different players are identified by player color / colored outer ring.

No Treasure slot exists on individual islands.
Treasure is drawn from one central Treasure Deck.

Board zones:

ZONE I
- 3 Mission Stack slots
- 2 Mission cards per stack
- 3 face-up choices at a time

ZONE II
- 3 Mission Stack slots
- 2 Mission cards per stack
- 3 face-up choices at a time

ZONE III
- 2 Mission Stack slots
- 2 Mission cards per stack
- 2 face-up choices at a time

FINAL ISLE
- 1 Final Mission Stack
- 5 Final Mission cards per game
- Drawn randomly from 8 available Final Missions
- Only the top Final Mission is active

SHARED AREAS
- Mission Discard
- Crew Deck / Crew Market
- Veteran / Elite recruitment area
- Treasure Deck
- Treasure Discard
- Gold supply
- Supply tokens
- Pirate Haven worker locations
- Player Pirate Tokens


======================================================================
2. MISSION STACK SETUP
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

ZONE I
1. Shuffle all 18 Zone I Mission cards.
2. Randomly draw 6.
3. Create 3 stacks of 2 cards.
4. Top card of each stack is face up.
5. Bottom card is face down.

ZONE II
1. Shuffle all 14 Zone II Mission cards.
2. Randomly draw 6.
3. Create 3 stacks of 2 cards.
4. Top card of each stack is face up.
5. Bottom card is face down.

ZONE III
1. Shuffle all 9 Zone III Mission cards.
2. Randomly draw 4.
3. Create 2 stacks of 2 cards.
4. Top card of each stack is face up.
5. Bottom card is face down.

FINAL ISLE
1. Shuffle all 8 Final Mission cards.
2. Randomly draw 5.
3. Stack all 5 in one pile.
4. Reveal only the top card.
5. Remaining 3 Final cards are not used this game.

When a normal Zone I–III Mission succeeds:
1. Apply the full Mission Reward.
2. Move that Mission to MISSION DISCARD.
3. If another card remains underneath that stack, reveal it.
4. If the stack is empty, reveal the board artwork:
   "MISSION COMPLETE"
5. Do not refill that stack from the unused Mission deck.

When a normal Zone I–III Mission fails:
- Mission stays in place
- Supply remains spent
- Lose 1 participating Crew unless prevented
- Mission can be attempted again later


======================================================================
3. GLOBAL ZONE PROGRESSION
======================================================================

[LOCKED – CURRENT WORKING RULE]

Zone II unlock:
- After 2 total successful Zone I Missions globally

Zone III unlock:
- After 3 total successful Zone II Missions globally

Final Isle unlock:
- After 1 total successful Zone III Mission globally

Progress is shared by all players.
One player's success can unlock a Zone for everyone.

Important:
After Final Isle is unlocked, Zone III remains available.
Players may continue completing Zone III Missions to gain Veteran,
Treasure, Supply or other upgrades before attempting Final Isle.

4. RESOURCES
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

GOLD
Used for:
- Recruiting Crew
- Buying Supply
- Some abilities
- Some Final Mission Favored Approaches

SUPPLY
Used to pay Expedition cost before a Mission begins.

Current Supply costs:
- Zone I     = 1 Supply
- Zone II    = 3 Supply
- Zone III   = 6 Supply
- Final Isle = 9 Supply

Supply is paid before rolling.
Normally, paid Supply is not returned on success or failure.
Card abilities may reduce, refund or gain Supply.

MARKET
- 1 Gold = 1 Supply
- Buy up to 5 Supply per Market action

CREW
- Maximum 4 participating Crew per Expedition
- Provide Power and abilities
- Normal Mission failure causes loss of 1 participating Crew unless prevented

VETERAN / ELITE CREW
- Higher-value Crew with stronger Power and/or abilities
- Important for late-game builds and several Final Mission conditions

TREASURE
- Active Treasure limit: 3
- Zone I and Zone II:
  Treasure abilities function, but Treasure gives no automatic Power
- Zone III and Final Isle:
  each active Treasure gives +1 Power
- Some Treasure may give additional Power or special effects

======================================================================

[LOCKED]

Custom six-sided Mission Die faces:

1. SKULL
2. 0
3. 0
4. 1
5. 2
6. GOLD

Result meanings:

SKULL
- Immediate Mission failure
- Cannot be rerolled, cancelled or modified

0
- +0 Power

1
- +1 Power

2
- +2 Power

GOLD
- +0 Power
- Gain 1 Gold
- Gold is still gained even if another die shows Skull

Dice by Zone:
- Zone I     = 1 die
- Zone II    = 2 dice
- Zone III   = 3 dice
- Final Isle = 3 dice in Final Mission Step 2

Multiple dice:
- If at least one Skull appears, Mission fails immediately

DICE CONTROL
- Maximum 1 Dice Control effect per roll
- Skull cannot be affected by Dice Control


======================================================================
7. POWER / MISSION RESOLUTION
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

Mission Total Power =

Participating Crew Power
+ Veteran / Elite Crew Power
+ Treasure Power when applicable
+ Numeric Dice Power
+ Ability bonuses
+ Final Edge when applicable

NORMAL MISSION SUCCESS
Success if:
1. No Skull is rolled
2. Total Power >= Mission Target

NORMAL MISSION FAILURE
Failure if:
- At least one Skull is rolled
OR
- Total Power < Target

MISSION RESOLUTION ORDER

1. Select an unlocked Zone
2. Select one face-up Mission
3. Select up to 4 participating Crew
4. Select up to 3 active Treasure
5. Apply pre-expedition effects / reductions
6. Pay Supply cost
7. Move Pirate Token to the Mission
8. Roll the required Mission Dice
9. Collect Gold-face rewards
10. Check Skull
11. Apply one permitted Dice Control effect if applicable
12. Calculate total Power
13. Resolve success or failure
14. Apply reward / loss / refund / card effects
15. Move Mission card if completed
16. Return Pirate Token to Pirate Haven

14. FINAL ISLE SYSTEM
======================================================================

[LOCKED]

Final Mission Pool:
8 cards

Game setup:
Randomly select 5 of the 8 Final Missions.

Final Supply Cost:
9 Supply

Every Final Mission:
- 2 Steps
- Step 1 = Favored Approach / build check
- Step 2 = Target 16 + roll 3 Mission Dice

FINAL EDGE
- If the player satisfies that Mission's Favored Approach:
  +1 Power in Step 2
- Maximum Final Edge = +1
- Final Edge does not stack
- A player may attempt the Final Mission without satisfying Favored Approach

FINAL SUCCESS
- If no Skull and Total Power >=16:
  player wins immediately

FINAL FAILURE
1. Supply 9 remains spent
2. Lose 1 Crew unless prevented
3. Move active Final Mission to Mission Discard
4. Reveal next Final Mission
5. Failed Final Mission can never be retried

FINAL DECK EMPTY
If the 5th Final Mission also fails:
- ALL PLAYERS LOSE immediately

16. FINAL BALANCE INTENT
======================================================================

[LOCKED DESIGN INTENT]

Every Final Mission has:
- Same Base Target = 16
- Same Step 2 Dice count = 3
- Different Step 1 preparation style

Reason:
Final difficulty should not depend on randomly drawing an easier Target.

Build identities:

Lost City of Oro
-> Treasure

Skull Fortress
-> Veteran / Crew sacrifice

Eye of the Storm
-> Supply / Veteran

Cursed Temple
-> Gold / Treasure sacrifice

Throne of the Pirate King
-> Balanced late-game build

Leviathan's Maw
-> Raw Crew Power

Queen of the Sirens
-> Crew diversity + Veteran

Black Horizon
-> Resource management / gamble

Intended late-game Final contender:
- 4 Crew
- approximately 2–3 Veteran
- approximately 2–3 Treasure
- enough Supply for Final cost
- total pre-dice Power typically around 14–15

Final Target 16 is intentionally high enough that:
- weak early rushes have poor odds
- Veteran and Treasure meaningfully improve win probability
- prepared players still face significant Skull risk

17. BALANCE TEST REFERENCE
======================================================================

[REFERENCE ONLY – SIMULATION, NOT HUMAN PLAYTEST]

Latest simulation changed only Supply costs from:
2 / 5 / 8 / 12
to:
1 / 3 / 6 / 9

Mission rewards remained v0.7.

Observed qualitative result:
- Market use dropped substantially
- Mission rewards became the main economy engine
- Veteran and Treasure remained important for winning Final
- Zone III remained useful after Final unlock
- P4 previously appeared advantaged when receiving an extra Crew
- v0.8 removes that extra P4 Crew and gives P4 the same bonus as P3

Previous simulated winning teams were approximately:
- Crew 4
- Veteran ~2.7
- Treasure ~2.5–2.7
- Pre-dice Final Power ~14.6

These numbers are simulation indicators only.
They should not be treated as verified human playtest statistics.
