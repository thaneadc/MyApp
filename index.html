📖 How to Play

The core rules at a glance.

Build your Crew

Recruit up to 4 Crew. Ready Crew contribute Combat, Sailing and Search. Exhausted Crew contribute no stats or abilities.

Explore the Sea

Zone I requires Sailing 1, Zone II Sailing 3, Zone III Sailing 5 and the Final Isle Sailing 6.

Roll Mission Dice

The custom die is 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3. Power 1–2 rolls 1 die, Power 3–5 rolls 2 dice and Power 6+ rolls 3 dice.

Win the Game

Be the first Captain to complete every stage of the Final Isle Mission during one Expedition.
